Identity & Foundation

Rooted in sisterhood, built for impact.

Aroham Foundation is an independent alumni initiative that bridges educational guidance and career empowerment through grassroots collaboration.

Our Roots

Founded by the alumni of Bolster Girls Campus (Parappur, Malappuram). Built on a deep culture of mutual upliftment, academic pursuit, and social responsibility.

Our Mission

Connecting students with mentors, guidance, and real-world opportunities through structured education, career counseling, and long-term community engagement.

Our Vision

Helping every student make informed academic and career decisions, breaking geographical and informational barriers for young women across Kerala and beyond.

Milestones

Our First-Year Journey

From the initial alumni spark in July 2025 to international guidance summits and future expansion.

12–13 July 2025 The Beginning

Envisioned at Bolster Girls Campus Alumni Meet

Aroham Foundation was conceived during the annual alumni gathering. The Executive Committee was officially constituted, and initial brainstorming sessions began.

29 July 2025 Digital Outreach

Official Digital Presence Established

Launch of the official Aroham Foundation Instagram channel, marking the beginning of our public communications and student engagement journey.

August – October 2025 Planning & Development

Foundational Branding & Governance Meetings

Regular online committee sessions to finalize the foundation name, emblem/logo, vision, mission, and long-term awareness campaigns.

22 October 2025 Public Launch

Official Campaign Launch

Broad public introduction to Aroham Foundation's purpose, inviting alumni and students to connect for mentorship and educational assistance.

25–29 December 2025 Strategic Alignment

HiLite Mall Strategic Session, Kozhikode

Strategic planning meeting held in Kozhikode. Planned future initiatives, structured the UniMates mentorship framework, and officially introduced the foundation's executive roadmap.

18 April 2026 Action Phase

GCC Higher Education Guidance Session

63 registrations, 50–55 active participants. International student speakers from the UAE and GCC.

4 May 2026 Flagship Launch

Official Launch of Aroham UniMates

Rollout of one-to-one mentorship matching. 17 initial student registrations paired with 10 registered alumni mentors.

10 May 2026 International

UK Higher Education Guidance Session

Conducted with Fida Haneef, guiding students on UCAS admissions, funding, and campus acclimatization.

23 May 2026 On-Ground Summit

EduWay 2026 · Rose Lounge, Malappuram

9 alumni mentors represented Aroham Foundation at the Future Skills & Career Summit, counseling Class 10–12 students across 9 disciplines.

25–26 July 2026 Inaugural Annual Summit

Bolster Girls Campus Alumni Meet 2026

Formal presentation of the 2025–2026 Inaugural Annual Report, reflection on our first-year milestones, and adoption of the 2026–2027 roadmap.

Core Learnings

  • Strong Teamwork Drives Outcomes: Dedicated volunteers and transparent communication overcome structural constraints.
  • Virtual Collaboration Works: High-quality mentorship can be delivered digitally to students in remote panchayats.
  • Every Program Builds Confidence: Positive participant response validates the immense need for accessible guidance.

Looking Ahead · Strategic Goals for 2026–2027

Objective 01

Expand UniMates

Grow the mentor network across new colleges, reaching more school students across Kerala districts.

Objective 02

Scholarship Cell

Help students identify and access state, central, and private financial aid opportunities to support higher education.

Objective 03

Strategic Partnerships

Build formalized collaborations with academic institutions, NGOs, and education professionals.

Objective 04

Regional Workshops

Organize interactive online and offline career guidance workshops across diverse high schools in Kerala.

Genesis & Evolution

Aroham - Our Story

From initial conversations at Bolster Girls Campus to a structured women-led alumni foundation.

Aroham Foundation began with a shared vision among the alumnae of Bolster Girls Campus to stay connected and create meaningful opportunities for the generations that follow.

The idea took shape during the Bolster Girls Campus Alumni Meet held on 12–13 July 2025. What began as conversations among alumnae gradually developed into a collective initiative. An executive committee was formed, and the initial discussions and planning to bring this vision to life began.

As the idea evolved, efforts were made to establish a distinct identity for the organisation. Between August and October 2025, the name, logo, vision and mission of Aroham were finalised, giving direction to the initiative and shaping its purpose. The official Instagram page had already been created on 29 July 2025, providing a platform to connect with the wider alumni community.

Key Strategic Milestone · HiLite Mall Kozhikode

On 22 October 2025, Aroham launched its awareness campaign to introduce the initiative and its vision to a wider audience. This was followed by a strategic planning meeting held from 25–29 December 2025 at HiLite Mall, Kozhikode, where further discussions and planning helped shape the organisation's future direction.

From those first conversations to the formation of an organised initiative, Aroham has grown through the collective efforts and commitment of its alumnae. Today, Aroham Foundation continues to work towards its vision of helping students make informed academic and career decisions through guidance, mentorship, education and community engagement.

Core Purpose

Mission

"Connecting students with mentors, guidance, and opportunities through education, career support, and community engagement."

1. Personalised Mentorship

Connecting higher secondary students with university alumnae through the UniMates one-to-one mentorship programme for tailored academic guidance.

2. Higher Education Guidance

Demystifying admissions, scholarships, and student life across national and international universities in the GCC, UK, and beyond.

3. Career Pathways

Providing on-ground summit guidance (EduWay) and workshops across medicine, engineering, architecture, design, sciences, and humanities.

4. Community Engagement

Mobilizing a vibrant network of 123+ alumni volunteers dedicated to educational equity and supporting young women across Kerala.

Long-Term Aspiration

Vision

"Helping every student make informed academic and career decisions."

Informed Decision-Making

Eliminating ambiguity in career choices by providing direct access to authentic experiences and unbiased academic perspectives.

Zero Information Barriers

Bridging the gap between students in rural panchayats and global educational opportunities through accessible virtual platforms.

Empowering Women Scholars

Nurturing self-belief, leadership qualities, and academic excellence among young women stepping into higher education.

Sustained Lifelong Sisterhood

Creating an enduring ecosystem where former mentees evolve into future mentors, perpetuating guidance for years to come.

One-to-One Student Mentorship Programme

Aroham UniMates

"Guidance from those who have been there. Support for those who are just beginning."

Aroham UniMates is a one-to-one student mentorship programme initiated by Aroham Foundation to connect students with alumnae mentors who can guide them in their academic and career journeys.

Personalised Academic & Career Guidance

Through personalised mentorship, students get the opportunity to interact with alumnae who share their experiences, knowledge and insights. The programme aims to help students explore different academic pathways, understand higher education opportunities and make informed decisions about their future.

Each student is connected with a mentor based on their areas of interest and the guidance they seek, creating a space where they can ask questions, gain clarity and learn from real experiences.

Programme Milestone

Our Beginning

Launched on 4 May 2026, Aroham UniMates began its journey with 10 registered mentors and 17 students. All 17 students were successfully matched with mentors, marking the beginning of a growing mentorship community.
